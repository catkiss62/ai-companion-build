# Roadmap after v0.29.1

## Immediate next stage: real proactive awareness

The next blocking product gap is no longer TTS. On the real Android 15 phone, Usage / Notification / Accessibility permissions exist, but the companion still feels unresponsive to ordinary phone activity.

Next work should tune the complete path:

`device event -> awareness observation -> perception -> meaningful Thought/Desire -> proactive gate -> notification / overlay / chat`

The goal is contextual companionship, not mechanical event narration.

## Then

1. HyperOS/Android 15 long-background and lock-screen behavior.
2. Long-memory stress/regression across many chat turns.
3. Phone/tablet Active Brain takeover on two real devices and `.aicomp` fallback.
4. Final stability and UX regression.

Minor TTS/UI imperfections remain eligible for later polish but are non-blocking after v0.29.1.
